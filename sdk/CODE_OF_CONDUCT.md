# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Ameba SDK project to be safe, respectful, and
productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
role.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their merits.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and move forward.
- Protect private information and security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's
private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, reviews, project-managed
community spaces, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/SPACE999978/ameba_sdk/security/advisories/new)
and identify the report as a conduct matter. Do not put sensitive conduct
reports in a public issue. The repository owner must enable that private channel
before the first public release.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently exclude participants when necessary to protect the community. They
should avoid conflicts of interest, limit report access to people handling the
matter, and explain an enforcement decision to the affected participant when
it is safe and appropriate to do so.
